### DAY 02 – Requirement Analysis

**Date:** __________

Today was the second day of my minor project development. Under the guidance of my project mentor, I started working on the requirement analysis phase of Veltrix AI. My mentor explained that before starting the actual coding process, it is important to understand the problem statement clearly and identify all the requirements of the proposed system.

During the discussion, my mentor guided me to identify the different types of users who could benefit from an AI-powered call reminder application. We discussed how people often forget important appointments, meetings, personal tasks and other daily activities due to their busy schedules. My mentor suggested that the application should provide an easy way to create reminders and notify users through automated voice calls.

I also discussed the functional requirements of the application, including creating reminders, managing contact details, processing user instructions through Google Gemini
and generating voice messages for automated calls. My mentor explained the difference between functional and non-functional requirements and guided me to consider factors 
such as usability, reliability, performance and security.

We further discussed the overall workflow of Veltrix AI. The user would provide a task through text or voice input, the AI model would understand the instruction, the 
application would store the required information in MongoDB Atlas, and the calling module would handle the reminder call using external services.

Based on my mentor's suggestions, I prepared an initial list of requirements that would serve as the foundation for the next stages of project development.

**Work Performed:**

- Discussed the requirement analysis phase with my project mentor.
- Identified the major problems faced by users while managing daily reminders and appointments.
- Defined the primary objective and expected functionality of Veltrix AI.
- Identified functional requirements such as task creation, contact management, AI-based instruction processing and automated voice calling.
- Identified non-functional requirements, including usability, reliability, performance and security.
- Discussed the role of Google Gemini in understanding natural-language instructions.
- Planned the use of MongoDB Atlas for storing contacts and reminder information.
- Explored the integration of Twilio for automated phone calls and ElevenLabs for voice generation.
- Discussed how users would interact with the application through text and voice input.
- Prepared an initial requirement list with the guidance of my mentor.

**Learning Outcome:**

Today, I understood the importance of requirement analysis in the software development life cycle. I learned how to identify functional and non-functional requirements and 
how to convert a real-world problem into specific software requirements. My mentor's guidance helped me understand the overall working process of Veltrix AI and provided a
clear direction for the upcoming development activities.
