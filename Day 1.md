DAY 01 – Project Introduction and Idea Selection

**Date:** __________

Today was the first day of my minor project development. Under the guidance of my project mentor, I started exploring different project ideas that could solve real-world 
problems using modern technologies. My mentor guided me to identify a problem that could be addressed through Artificial Intelligence and automation.

After discussing various ideas, I selected an AI-powered Call Reminder Agent named **Veltrix AI**. My mentor suggested developing a system that could help users remember 
important tasks, appointments and daily activities by automating reminder calls.

During the discussion, my mentor explained how artificial intelligence could be used to understand user instructions and how voice technology could make the application 
more interactive and convenient. I also discussed the possibility of allowing users to enter reminders through text or voice commands.

My mentor guided me to focus on developing a practical application rather than just a theoretical AI-based project. We also discussed the basic working process of the system,
its target users and the technologies that could be used for implementation.

**Work Performed:**

- Explored different minor project ideas with the guidance of my mentor.
- Discussed real-world problems that could be solved using Artificial Intelligence.
- Identified the difficulty of remembering important tasks, appointments and daily activities.
- Selected the project title **Veltrix AI – AI-Powered Call Reminder Agent**.
- Discussed the basic working concept and objectives of the application.
- Explored the possibility of using AI to understand natural-language instructions.
- Discussed voice-based reminders and automated phone calls.
- Received initial guidance regarding the technologies and tools required for development.
- Prepared an initial plan for the upcoming development activities.

**Learning Outcome:**

I understood how to identify a real-world problem and convert it into a practical software project. I also learned about the potential use of Artificial Intelligence and
voice automation in developing an intelligent reminder system. Based on my mentor's suggestions, I finalized the initial concept of Veltrix AI and planned to proceed with 
requirement analysis in the next session.
